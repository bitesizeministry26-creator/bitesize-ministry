Discover Bite Size Serve With Us

Welcome to Bite Size 💛

Every child deserves a place where they are known, loved, encouraged and given the opportunity to grow in Christ.

🌱 Our Vision

To develop children ages 3–12 into lifelong disciples of Jesus Christ.

✨ Our Philosophy

We present Christ in meaningful, age-appropriate and memorable ways that children can understand, apply and carry with them beyond Children's Church.

What We Believe

Our ministry is built around three simple commitments.

👀

Every Child Known

We intentionally know the children we serve.

💛

Every Child Loved

Every child should experience the love of Christ.

🌱

Every Child Growing

We help children grow in faith, character and purpose.
