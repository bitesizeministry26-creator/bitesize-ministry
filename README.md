## 🌟 About Bite Size Ministry

Bite Size Ministry is the Children's Ministry of Escarpment Road New Testament Church of God, committed to helping children encounter Christ, discover their God-given identity, develop their gifts, and grow into lifelong disciples of Jesus Christ.

Our ministry seeks to create a welcoming, organized, engaging, and Christ-centered environment where every child is seen, valued, loved, and given opportunities to grow.

### Our Philosophy

**Christ in Bite-Sized Pieces.**

We believe that the truth of God's Word can be presented in meaningful, age-appropriate, and memorable ways that children can understand, apply, and carry with them beyond the classroom.

---

## 🎯 Our Vision

To develop children ages 3–12 into lifelong disciples of Jesus Christ.
